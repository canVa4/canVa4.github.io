Email  /  GitHub  /  Google Scholar  

Research

My research focuses on embodied AI and robot learning, with a particular interest in leveraging human data to improve robot manipulation. Recently, I have been exploring Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, World-Action Models (WAMs), and cross-embodiment learning to enable more generalizable robot policies through human-robot knowledge transfer. I am also interested in dexterous manipulation, humanoid robotics, reinforcement learning, and sim-to-real transfer.
